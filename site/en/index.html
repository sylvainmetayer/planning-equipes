Is the rota legal?

Yes. The solver applies ten articles of the French Labour Code and cannot break them. When it refuses an assignment, it cites the article.

  • Daily hours L3121-18
  • Weekly hours L3121-20
  • Breaks L3121-16
  • Daily rest L3131-1
  • Weekly rest L3132-2
  • Under-18s: hours L3162-1
  • Under-18s: breaks L3162-3
  • Under-18s: nights L3163-1
  • Under-18s: rest L3164-2
  • Under-18s: public holidays L3164-6

Read the rules in the code (in French) · The software helps you decide. The organisation that publishes the rota remains the employer.

Questions we get asked

What if the solver finds no solution?

It shows which rule is blocking, on which shifts and for whom. Usually someone is just missing on a Saturday evening. The staffing check flags it before the first run.

What happens to our staff's data?

It stays with you, and we only ask for what scheduling needs. The date of birth is only used to know who is under 18 on the day of a shift. The AI assistant never sees it, and team chats only get numbers, never names.

Do we need someone technical?

Not if we host it for you. Everything happens in the browser, in English or French, and your staff install nothing.
