### MCP tools of the stands, the staff, the availabilities and the timeslots

- **Before (3.x)**: one tool per gesture on each record. A stand was read by `lister_stands` or `consulter_stand`, created by `creer_stand` or `creer_stand_complet`, and its opening hours went through four tools (a plage, its removal, a recurring rule, clearing the model). A staff record was read by `lister_animateurs` or `consulter_animateur`. Availabilities had three reads (`consulter_collecte_disponibilites`, `lister_declarations_disponibilite`, `lister_demandes_covoiturage`) and two decisions (`appliquer_declaration_disponibilite`, `refuser_declaration_disponibilite`). The timeslot grid was described by `diagnostiquer_grille_creneaux`, checked by `valider_creneaux`, and deleted by `supprimer_creneau` or `supprimer_creneaux`.
- **After (4.0)**: one tool per object and per verb, the sub-object or the part read passed as an argument ([ADR 0127](repo:docs/decisions/0127-un-outil-mcp-par-objet-et-par-verbe.md)). `lister_stands` and `lister_animateurs` take an `id` for a single record. `creer_stand` also creates the location, the missing game categories and the opening windows, as `creer_stand_complet` did. A stand's opening hours are one object: `creer_horaire_stand` and `supprimer_horaire_stand`, with `forme` = `plage` (one dated day) or `modele` (the recurring model). `consulter_disponibilites` reads the Availability screen by `section` (`collecte`, `declarations` or `covoiturage`), and `modifier_declaration_disponibilite` decides a declaration (`decision` = `appliquer` or `refuser`). Opening the collection stays `configurer_collecte_disponibilites`, apart, because it may write to the staff. `diagnostiquer_creneaux` reads the grid by `section` (`grille` or `controle`), and `supprimer_creneaux` takes `ids` or filters. These families go from 36 tools to 26. Locations and game categories keep their four tools each.
- **What you do**: nothing right away. The 11 old names of 3.x stay published until 5.0, with their arguments and their answers, and their description starts with « Ancien nom ». Three plage tools born while 4.0 was being prepared (`creer_plage_stand`, `supprimer_plage_stand`, `supprimer_horaires_stand`) go without an alias: no published version carried them. A prompt, an instruction or a script that names an old tool must move to the new names before 5.0: the mapping is in [the old tool names](../comprendre/mcp-outils.md#anciens-noms-doutils).
