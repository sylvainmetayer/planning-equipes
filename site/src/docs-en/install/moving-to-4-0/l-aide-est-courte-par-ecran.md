### The help is short per screen, and What's new is a list of versions

- **Before (3.x)**: the toolbar's "?" icon opened a section of the user guide, a long one, chosen per page: a tab such as *Staffing need* or *Plan versions* landed on the section of the whole page, and several screens had no help at all. *What's new* offered a search and a filter by kind, and the detail of every commit.
- **After (4.0)**: every page of the menu, and every tab that answers a question of its own, has its short help: what the screen answers, how to do its main gesture, and the link to the site's guide. The "?" icon opens the one of the tab shown. The detailed sections of the user guide remain, linked from the short help; "How to do without a forced assignment" says how to keep someone on a timeslot (a lock) or rule them out for a day (an unavailable day on their sheet). *What's new* becomes the list of versions, newest first, each with its number, its date and its highlights; an `-rc.N` pre-release is marked "Pre-release". The filter and the search are gone (the `type` and `q` parameters of an old link are ignored), the full detail staying in the release notes.
- **What you do**: nothing. No address breaks, and no data is touched.
