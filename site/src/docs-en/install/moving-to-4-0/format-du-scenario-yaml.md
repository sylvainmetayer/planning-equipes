### YAML scenario format

- **Before**: the application writes `schemaVersion: 1`.
- **After**: it writes `schemaVersion: 7`. It still reads v1 files (key absent or equal to 1): they go through a chain of conversions, link by link. What the chain cannot convert is ignored, and every ignored item appears in the import report's warnings, never silently. The 1 → 2 link renames the game categories' `ninja` key to `polyvalent`, losing nothing; the 2 → 3 link moves `parametresLegaux` and `parametresQualite` under `parametres:` (`legal` and `organisation`) and ignores `parametresSolveur` (see [The edition's settings in four sections](#les-paramètres-de-lédition-en-quatre-sections)); the 3 → 4 link converts nothing, it opens `contraintes.niveaux` (see [The level of an organisation rule](#le-niveau-dune-règle-dorganisation-se-choisit-par-édition)); the 4 → 5 link converts the stands' opening hours into plages (see [Stand opening hours](#horaires-des-stands)); the 5 → 6 link renames the `journeesTypes` section to `gabarits` (see [One timeslot generator](#un-seul-générateur-de-créneaux)); the 6 → 7 link ignores the `couverturePause` key (see [The reduced-staffing meal relay is removed](#le-relais-repas-à-effectif-réduit-est-retiré)). Whatever its version, a `contraintes:` key naming a rule merged or removed in 4.0 is read under the name that replaces it, on the most protective reading, and the import report says so (see [Schedule rule names](#noms-des-règles-du-planning)). Likewise, whatever its version, locks of a removed type, forced ad hoc exceptions and the hard form of the days in a row are converted on reading, with a warning (see [The lock has only three types](#le-verrou-na-plus-que-trois-types), [The forced assignment and the forced unavailability](#laffectation-forcée-et-lindisponibilité-forcée-sont-converties) and [One days-in-a-row rule](#une-seule-règle-de-jours-daffilée)).
- **What you do**: nothing to read your old files, end-of-event archives or copies taken before a wipe. A v2 to v7 scenario cannot be imported into a 3.x application ("newer format"): keep a v1 export before upgrading, it is your way back. The v1 reader is removed in 5.0.
