### The Planning page offers only four views

- **Before (3.x)**: the Planning page mixed two address keys. `?axe=jour|stand|personne|typologie` chose the axis, and `?vue=` the rendering of that axis (`calendrier`, `rail`, `carte`, `pauses`, `changements` for the day; `treemap`, `frise`, `synthese` for the grids). It also offered "Compare with...", which laid a second day beside the first.
- **After (4.0)**: four views, one key, `?vue=jour|stand|personne|carte`. "Day" keeps its two modes (`mode=rail|tableau`). "Stand" and "Person" are the two grids of the event; their own rendering (`treemap` for the hours breakdown, `frise` and `synthese` for people) moves to `?rendu=`. **Four readings leave the page**: "Compare two days" is removed; "By game category" is the *Typology* section of the *Staffing need* tab of the Diagnostic; the breaks without relay are read in *Diagnostic*, *Problems* tab; what moved since the last publication is read in *Publish*, *Send* tab, and what the last solve rewrote in the result of the *Calculate* tab. The screen planned for the breaks ("Meals and breaks") will come in 4.x; until then, the old address leads to the screen that holds it today.
- **What you do**: nothing for your links. Until 5.0, `?axe=stand|personne` and the grid's `vue` (`treemap`, `frise`, `synthese`) are read as before and rewritten to the 4.0 form; `?axe=typologie` leads to the *Typology* section of the Diagnostic's *Staffing need*, `?vue=pauses` to *Problems*, `?vue=changements` to *Publish* (the day of `date` kept), and `/pauses`, `/intendance` and `/typologies-planning` to the same places. `?comparer=` and its keys (`ecarts`) are ignored. A bookmark or an internal help page that named one of these views should be moved to its new screen before 5.0, which will remove the old keys.
