### The menu holds nine entries

- **Before (3.x)**: the side menu filed twelve screens under six folding groups (*Home*, *Prepare*, *Plan*, *Share*, *Today*, *Administer*). *Editions*, *Edition measures* and *Debug* were not in it: they opened from "Manage", from the Ctrl+K palette or from their address.
- **After (4.0)**: nine entries, in the order of the cycle: **Home**, **Prepare** (Stands, Calendar, Staff), **Files**, **Planning** (with its *Calculate* tab), **Diagnostic**, **Rules**, **Share** (Share the planning, Swaps), **Today** and **Administer** (Settings, Editions, Edition measures, Debug). An entry that is one page is a link; *Prepare*, *Share* and *Administer* fold over their pages. *Debug* is offered to the administrator account only, as in the palette; a read-only account sees the same entries as everybody else. The "g" then a letter shortcuts do not change, and the palette still knows every screen and every tab.
- **What you do**: nothing for your links. No address changes: a bookmark opens the same page as in 3.x. *Prepare*, *Share* and *Administer* keep the folded or unfolded state you gave them in 3.x; *Plan* and the *Today* group are gone, and their state is forgotten.
