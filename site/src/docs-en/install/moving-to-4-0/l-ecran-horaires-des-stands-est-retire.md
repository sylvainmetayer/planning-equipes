### The "Stand hours" screen and the rule analysis are removed

- **Before (3.x)**: the *Stand hours* tab of the *Calendar* (the former *Openings* screen) showed a stand × timeslot grid, entered and reviewed in the same place, with its views by day template, by combined calendar (the layers of an opening) and the comparison of two to eight stands. The analysis also flagged three points about how the recurring rules were written: two rules that overlap, a rule no day reads, two windows of one rule at different headcounts.
- **After (4.0)**: those screens no longer exist. A stand's hours are read and entered on its **fiche** (its model and its dated windows); the solve reads only windows, so overlaps between the model's rules are no longer flagged (the model editor only says which rule takes over which). The old addresses (`/ouvertures`, `/creneaux?onglet=horaires`) open the fiche of the stand they name (`stand`, or the first of `stands`), otherwise the stands list. The fiche no longer carries opening anomalies: the number of open days and seats stays, and the anomalies that still mean something (a stand never open, a window outside every timeslot, a stretch too short) are counted on the home page, in the calendar check and in the referential coherence.
- **What you do**: nothing for your data. Tell the organisers the grid is gone. On the API side, **no alias is kept**: `GET /api/ouvertures-stands/couches` no longer exists, `horaireId` leaves the anomalies, `informations` leaves `ouvertures` in the edition state, and the codes `REGLES_CHEVAUCHANTES`, `REGLE_MASQUEE` and `FENETRES_CHEVAUCHANTES` are no longer described (they had not been raised since the move to windows). `GET /api/ouvertures-stands` and `PUT /api/ouvertures-stands/grille` stay, the first for the stands list, the fiche and the calendar, the second for the guided creation of a stand. On the assistant side, the `analyser_ouvertures_stands` tool is removed (see [the old tool names](../comprendre/mcp-outils.md#anciens-noms-doutils)): `lister_anomalies_referentiel`, `STANDS` family, gives its anomalies. The "Compact the hours" action had already gone with the move to windows.
