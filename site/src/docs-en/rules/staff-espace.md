# The staff espace

You know what each staff member sees and can do in their personal espace, with no account to create, and what you control on your side.

A staff member has no account. They receive a personal link to their **espace** and identify themselves with a code received by e-mail. The espace is open only for **the active edition** (see [Chain editions](enchainer-les-editions.md#activer-une-édition)). You copy this link from the person's profile or send it to them with their schedule (see [Publish the schedule](publier-planning.md)).

On the organiser's side, [what each team member receives](/organiser-son-equipe/) summarises the same journey.

## What they find there

- their schedule, their rest days;
- their swap requests (see [Let staff swap their timeslots](echanger-creneaux.md));
- their declared availability, during the collection (see [Collect availability](collecter-disponibilites.md));
- the organisation's phone number and e-mail address, at the foot of every page. This contact is set per edition, in Settings, *Edition* tab; a duplicated edition keeps it;
- a "Help" tab, written for the staff member and not for the organisation: reading their schedule, taking it with them, requesting a swap and following what becomes of it, in folded questions readable by finger on a phone.

## On a phone, during the event

The espace is read standing up, on a phone. Three tabs:

- the **day**: a strip of days to scroll with the thumb, the state of the moment, one card per timeslot with its place, its route and its teammates;
- the **overview**: four figures and a timeline of all the days, coloured by stand game category;
- the **teammates**: "Am I with anyone?", by name, accents and case ignored. Days when almost the whole headcount is present give only a number.

Proposing a swap stays within thumb's reach under the three tabs.

At the top, before the tabs: the current or next seat, the confirmation while it is due, what changed, and a single button to take their schedule with them. Navigation fits on one row; "My availability" and "Carpool" only appear during the collection, which a banner announces. "Schedule communicated on" is shown only if the e-mail really went out, and otherwise the espace says so.

### The marker of the day

During the event, the espace opens on the present moment: the day's date, the current or next seat with the break still to be taken, the cards of past seats faded but not gone. A night seat stays "in progress" after midnight. Before the first day and after the last, the espace opens on the first day and says nothing about "now".

### Where my stand is

Each seat, and each break, names the location of its stand when it has one, and opens it on a map when it is geolocated. The calendar file and the PDF say it too.

## What changed for you

Above their days, the staff member rereads the exact sentences of the last publication that moved their schedule: the same as their e-mail, folded behind a title that gives their number. A lost message or one filed as spam therefore does not erase the trace of what moved. A first distribution announces a schedule and not a list of corrections, and the banner goes to the background once presence is confirmed. Swap requests stay on their tab, with their status of the moment.

## "I will not be able to come"

From their espace, over the whole edition (fair and collection closed included), the staff member reports that they will not be able to be there, on a day or on a seat, with an optional reason chosen from a short list.

- You are notified at once by e-mail.
- Nothing changes in the schedule until you have noted the absence and replaced, or filed the report.
- The staff member rereads what they reported and where it stands, cancels it as long as nothing is decided, and receives an e-mail when you decide.

Offered by default, this action can be disabled for an edition: it then disappears from the espaces, along with the reports already received.

## Emergency contact and legal representative

Each staff member can indicate, in their espace (*My contacts*), whom to notify in case of illness and, for a minor, their legal representative (name and phone; everything is optional). You (administrators and the *HR* right) read them and can enter them. A minor without a representative is flagged on entry and in the compliance report.

This third-party data appears in no export, no scenario, no assistant tool and not in a colleague's espace, and does not carry over from one edition to the next.

## The staff member's calendar

They can download their schedule in ICS format, or subscribe to a permanent address that their calendar updates at each republication. They replace this address themselves in one click if it has leaked. See [Publish the schedule](publier-planning.md#envoyer-les-documents).
