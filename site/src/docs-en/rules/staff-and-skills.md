# Enter staff and their skills

You describe your team: identity, skills by game category, wishes, unavailability, and you find everything known about a person on a single profile.

People under 18 fall under a separate regime: [what having minor staff members changes](/animateurs-mineurs/) is explained in short questions, with the articles.

## Add a staff member

1. Open *Staff* and add a person.
2. Fill in the identity, the skills by game category and level, the wishes, the unavailability days, the e-mail and the phone number.
   - E-mail and phone are optional. The phone number is only used to call a replacement on the event day; it never appears on the wall display, a schedule or an export.
   - The legal regime (minor or adult) is derived from the age on the date of each timeslot: there is nothing to tick.
3. In the form, one click on a day of the edition's timeline marks it as absent, and an "absent from ... to ..." range is set in one gesture.

To enter the levels of the whole team at once, use the skills grid (*Skills* tab of the Staff page), described below.

For a team already in a spreadsheet, the CSV import saves the typing: see [Import and export files](importer-exporter.md#importer-vos-animateurs-depuis-un-tableur).

## Read the staff list

The list shows age and regime, skills as badges, unavailability and, once a schedule is solved, each person's seats. It can be filtered on minors, managers, a skill or a wish, each filter showing as a chip. Two filters serve the reminders (never confirmed, silent for N days): see [Publish the schedule](publier-planning.md#relancer-ceux-qui-nont-pas-confirmé).

## Enter all ratings at once: the skills grid

*Staff*, *Skills* tab. The grid is the only place where ratings are entered in bulk: a person's profile only edits their own. It is not exchanged by file.

- Staff members in rows, game categories in columns.
- A cell changes on click or with the 0 to 3 keys.
- The arrows move around as in a spreadsheet. A row can copy the one above, and a cell can be applied to its whole column.
- The wish is set or removed in the same cell, by clicking the heart or pressing S, and is saved with the rest.
- A block copied from a spreadsheet is pasted from the current cell, after a preview.
- Nothing is written before "Save". Each profile keeps its protection against simultaneous edits.

## A staff member's profile

The profile gathers everything known about a person, and what is done for them. Every staff member name in the application leads to it. Previous and next move through the staff list as you filtered it.

From the profile, you can:

- copy the link to their espace, e-mail them their schedule, remind them;
- download their PDF or their ICS file;
- lock or release their whole schedule, add an adjustment already in their name;
- edit their skills and wishes in place.

The profile also shows:

- their schedule day by day (opening span, shifts, gaps, breaks, travel), each shift leading to the Planning page;
- their load and fairness, with a radar and "Compare with...";
- their fragility (lock, train) and their follow-up.

### Declare an absence from the profile

One click on a day of the timeline makes the person unavailable. In the same gesture, their seats for that day are released in the saved schedule, and each one is immediately offered to a replacement ("Who can hold this seat?"). A locked seat stays in place: the profile names it, with the link to the locks. The absence can be cancelled without giving the seats back.
